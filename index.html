Embodied Evaluation Observatory

Statistics for published robot evaluations · data health and sample budget

We do not evaluate robots. We check the numbers evaluations publish: whether the spread between runs is real, and how many attempts a claim needs before its interval means anything. Dashboard.

We take published evaluation records, measure how much of the variation in them is repetition rather than capability, and report what a record can and cannot support. We produce no robot performance data of our own.

Protocol

Method and reporting obligations: the protocol specification, version 1.2. The protocol states what we publish each release, what we check in a source, and five commitments — including that we never delete a record and never sell a rank.

Licence
