# Contribution record — Zimo Li

## What he contributed

He built a simulation of a precision insertion task after a public evaluation showed a
general-purpose model succeeding at coarse manipulation and failing at fine assembly. His
first result was that the failure occurred before contact, where the parts were not aligned
and no useful force signal existed yet. From there the question changed from which design
performs better to whether two reported numbers describe the same thing at all.

That question is the reason this archive exists.

## Where it is visible

The framing is in `COMPARABILITY.md` and `ATTRIBUTION.md`; the gap statement that the
archive is built around is in `reda-protocol.md` §3. Those are the only places a reader
should look.
