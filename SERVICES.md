# Complementary work

The archive itself is free and stays free: published tables, scripts, certificates and
the protocol. This page lists work that can be commissioned alongside it, so that the
line between published evidence and paid assistance is visible.

## 1. Data quality assistance

A benchmark or vendor supplies its evaluation records; the same export and verify
pipeline is run against them before publication, and the report states cell counts,
thin-cell share, correlation, and the zero-failure bound the data can support. The
benchmark decides what to publish. The archive does not edit the numbers and does not
withhold a result.

## 2. Claim verification assistance

A vendor statement — "first", "89% success", "zero teleoperation" — is checked against
the record the vendor is willing to show: which benchmark, which cells, how many
attempts, whether repeats were counted. Output is a certificate at the level the
evidence supports (L1, L2 or L3, see `CERTIFICATE_LEVELS.md`), including the case where
the claim cannot be supported at any level.

## 3. Deployment evidence review

For a deployed fleet, the 17-field deployment template is filled from operational
records and audited: Wilson interval on the success rate, intervention rate, and an
explicit check that the denominator is stated and interventions count as failures.
Output describes the deployment record, not the robot.

## Boundaries

- No paid ranking, no sponsored tier position, no delaying a release for a client.
- Commissioned work is disclosed in the release it touches.
- A commissioned certificate uses the same layers and the same wording as a
  self-initiated one; being paid changes who asked, not what is verified.
- No safety certification, no regulatory opinion, no warranty.
