# Audit note — arXiv:2610.12369V1 (COAP), RoboDojo table

Date: 2026-11-20. Archive release used for comparison: 2026-11-17 and 2026-11-19 (RoboDojo subset).
Subject: Hu, Hu, Hong, Chen, Liu, *Embodied Turing Machines: Stateful Code for Robot Recursive
Self-Improvement*, arXiv:2610.12369v1, 8 Oct 2026.

## Reachable level

The paper is not in the archive and publishes no per-episode records: Table 2 gives five
domain aggregates and one overall figure per method. What can be checked is the arithmetic
between those aggregates and the reported overall, plus the uncertainty the aggregates imply.
That is an aggregate-level check, not a reproduction: nothing here confirms that 70.24 came
from the 6,300 episodes the paper describes.

## What the overall number is

For every method, the reported Overall equals the unweighted mean of the five domain success
rates, not an episode-weighted rate. 24 of 24 rows reproduce to within 0.03 pp.

The five domains hold unequal task counts (Memory 6, Precision 8, Open 8, Generalization 12,

Largest divergences under task weighting: PhysicalRSI −2.23 pp, DM0.5 −2.14 pp,
SimpleMemVLA −1.66 pp, COAP −1.37 pp. The macro choice is defensible as a statement about
five domains, but it is not the success rate of a randomly chosen RoboDojo task, and the
paper's headline is quoted without that qualifier.

## Uncertainty implied by the aggregates

Treating the five domain rates as five clusters: COAP SD 12.85 pp, SE 5.74 pp,
95% interval ±11.3 pp, i.e. 70.24 ∈ [59.0, 81.5].

Applying the archive's own RoboDojo ICC (rho = 0.5013, release 2026-11-17) to the paper's
stated design — 42 tasks, 3 seeds, 50 layouts, 6,300 episodes, 150 per task — gives
deff = 75.7, effective n = 83.2, interval ±9.8 pp. The two routes agree
to about a point, which is the useful part: the aggregate route is not optimistic.

Rank stability: over 276 method pairs, 197 (71%) have overlapping
95% intervals. Simate-beta 27.96 and Rex-M1 27.44 are indistinguishable; so are VPP2
25.62 and Liber-0 Preview 25.52. COAP separates from every baseline (lower bound
59.0 against the best baseline upper bound 41.7),
so the headline gap is not in doubt; the ordering inside the VLA cluster is.

## Comparability with the archive

| Property | This paper | Archive (RoboDojo subset) |
|---|---|---|
| tasks | 42 | 6 |
| episodes | 6,300 (3 seeds × 50 layouts per task) | 2,151 attempts |
| success definition | binary, whole task succeeds | score 0 / partial / complete |
| mean outcome | SR 70.24 | score 0.3338; complete 9.7%, partial 65.5%, zero 24.9% |
| grading | single protocol | 5 regimes (82% autograded, 15% report score, 1% human label, 1% re-graded, 0.2% timeout) |

The two numbers cannot be placed on one axis: different task subsets, and binary success
against partial credit. The archive's 0.3338 is the mean progress score over six tasks, not
the success share over 42, so no discrepancy is being alleged and none can be. What the
archive can say is only this: on the six tasks it holds, the same benchmark yields a very
different distribution of outcomes from the one the headline summarises.

## One cross-check that does not work

The archive holds GPT-6 Astra on the six RoboDojo tasks it covers: mean progress score 0.3961,
whole-task complete 8.3% of 240 attempts. The paper reports GPT-6 Astra at 22.48 overall.
Neither number contradicts the other, and neither explains the other: 42 tasks against 6,
binary success against partial credit. Recording the attempt was worthwhile only because it
shows where the mapping fails; it is not evidence against the paper.

## Verdict

- Arithmetic: consistent. Overall reproduces as the macro mean of five domains in all 24 rows.
- Interval: 70.24 carries roughly ±11 pp at 95% from the paper's own aggregates, ±10 pp from
  the archive's ICC applied to its design.
- Reproduction: not possible. No per-episode records, no per-task table, no code version
  tied to the reported run.
- To become auditable: publish per-task or per-episode records (task id, seed, layout id,
  outcome, grading rule) and the library version used; then the archive's ingest path
  (`tools/export_public.py`, `tools/verify_public.py`) can carry it as a board.
