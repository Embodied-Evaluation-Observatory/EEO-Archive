2 · Sample budget

A cell cannot be made precise by repeating it. The effective count a cell can ever reach is 1/rho. At the completion family's 0.50 that is 2 independent observations, however many attempts go into the cell. Precision has to come from cells, and the arithmetic below says how many.

Attempts saturate

Ten attempts per cell already reach 90% of what a cell can deliver at rho≈0.50; the current median is 20. Beyond the saturation point, extra attempts inside one cell buy almost nothing.

Cells needed for a target half-width

At p≈0.5, independent observations needed are z20.25/h2; cells needed are that count times rho, because each cell is worth 1/rho. These are arithmetic on the measured rho, not new estimates.

A claim good to ±2 points needs about 1,200 cells at rho≈0.50 — the reason this archive reports intervals instead of ranking models.

A budget is only a budget if it is written before the run; one produced afterwards is a precision assessment. A one-fleet design that fits in a week: 150 attempts, three task types, five configurations.

3 · Reproduction

The published tables follow from the published attempts, and this is checkable from a clean clone. What the archive cannot issue is the outside run: a check performed by someone who does not hold the key. The request and the procedure are in REPRODUCTION_INVITATION.md.

python3 tools/verify_public.py --release 2026-11-19 --input releases/2026-11-17/robocurve_allruns.json
python3 tools/preflight.py --release 2026-11-19 --input releases/2026-11-17/robocurve_allruns.json --zenodo-dir <deposit copy>

The first recomputes the summary from the attempts and exits zero on agreement. The second adds the deposit check; without a local copy of the deposit it blocks that item by design and says so. governance/verification_partners.csv carries only its header: no outside run has been recorded, and the file is not filled in with friendly-sounding entries.

Source-side checks

Five submission checks, run on the 2026-11-17 snapshot. Three are computable from published data, one is partial, one is not implemented. A flag never deletes a record.

Status

  • computedShare concentration — top three models hold 8.3% of RoboTwin trials
  • computedTemporal clustering — 769 RoboChallenge cells with timestamps; 10 in the busiest hour, 15 sharing a minute
  • computedPairwise consistency — 192 RoboTwin pairs agree on every shared task
  • partialSelective submission — 32,980 episodes against 769 cells, 42.9 per cell; per-cell counts unstated
  • absentContamination probe — no perturbed task variant has been run

What the flags do not say

A high concentration share can be a single large lab publishing many runs. A batch of submissions in one hour can be one evaluation sweep. Perfect pairwise consistency can mean the tasks are too easy to separate models. Each pattern is reported with its reading, not as a verdict.
