What limits these numbers

Two limits travel with every figure above. They are reported, not corrected away.

Provenance card

Five submission checks. Three are computable from published data, one is partial, one is not implemented. A flag never deletes a record.

Status

  • computedShare concentration — top three models hold 8.3% of RoboTwin trials
  • computedTemporal clustering — 769 RoboChallenge cells with timestamps; 10 in the busiest hour, 15 sharing a minute
  • computedPairwise consistency — 192 RoboTwin pairs agree on every shared task
  • partialSelective submission — 32,980 episodes against 769 cells, 42.9 per cell; per-cell counts unstated
  • absentContamination probe — no perturbed task variant has been run

What the flags do not say

A high concentration share can be a single large lab publishing many runs. A batch of submissions in one hour can be one evaluation sweep. Perfect pairwise consistency can mean the tasks are too easy to separate models. Each pattern is reported with its reading, not as a verdict.
